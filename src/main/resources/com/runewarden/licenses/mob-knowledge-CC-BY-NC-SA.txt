Monster reference data

mob-knowledge.json contains a reduced factual snapshot of the OSRS Wiki DPS calculator monster data, by OSRS Wiki contributors and the weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc contributors.

Source: https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/a27fc3c898eb15accbfa0457c89b6b6a1b132f1f/cdn/json/monsters.json

Adaptation: retained NPC IDs, variant names, attack-style descriptions, size, magic level, magic attack bonus, defensive bonuses, elemental vulnerability and monster attributes. Images, descriptive prose and calculator implementation are omitted. Duplicate NPC-ID variants remain separate in this data; the application merges reference descriptions conservatively and rejects inconsistent calculator stats.

The fixed usual-protection reference maps documented single attack types to the corresponding protection prayer; this is an application inference from the source attack types, not a current-attack read. Supplementary exceptions and rules were verified from revision-pinned OSRS Wiki pages on 16 October 2026. Full page titles, revision IDs and URLs are listed in mob-knowledge.json under protectionReferenceSources. These include Protection prayers, Bloodveld, Jelly, Kraken, Enormous Tentacle, Verzik Vitur, Zulrah/Strategies, Phantom Muspah, Vardorvis and Tombs of Amascut/Strategies. Mixed attackers retain the complete documented combination. No source walkthrough text or images are copied.
