Runewarden Agility reference, retrieved 17 October 2026.

Source: RuneLite client 1.13.2 sources JAR.
https://github.com/runelite/runelite/tree/runelite-parent-1.13.2/runelite-client/src/main/java/net/runelite/client/plugins/agility
Courses.java course XP/endpoints and Obstacles.java identifier sets are adapted into AgilityCourseCatalog/AgilityObstacles. Lap completion follows positive native Agility XP at the published end points, or native Colossal Wyrm progress reaching 6. Brimhaven ticket movement countdown follows AgilityArenaTimer's 60 seconds. Public AgilityShortcut matching/requirements remain provided by the installed RuneLite client.
