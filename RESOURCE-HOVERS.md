# Resource hover cards

Hover a supported resource in the inventory or bank to see its gathering/production source, named uses, required levels, and base XP for each action. Hold Shift for recipe quantities and method notes. Actions above the player's current level use a warning colour. The original item menus and clicks remain unchanged.

The catalog covers every ingredient in all 196 banked-XP recipes, the pinned RuneLite 1.13.2 Woodcutting, Mining and Fishing actions, and common herb seeds and tree seeds/saplings. Noted items use ItemManager.canonicalize. Unknown items have no card. Items hidden by bank scrolling, open context menus and dragged items do not show cards.

XP is labelled per action, with the quantity of the hovered ingredient consumed by that action. Gathering XP and processing XP are listed independently. This matters for resources such as yew logs: chopping, burning and fletching give different XP. Recipe notes preserve special requirements, including lit altar burners and potion ingredients. Seed and sapling XP comes from Wiki Farming info records: planting allocation, checking health and harvesting are named separately. Herb planting allocation is credited with the first harvest. Per-plant revision URLs are retained.

The compact card shows up to four named actions; Shift reveals details for up to six. Cards also cap their rows to the current canvas height. Source summaries describe gathering or processing origins rather than an exhaustive monster drop table. Location-specific unlocks can still apply.

Data uses the existing bundled banked-xp-recipes.json (196 actions, with RuneLite 1.13.2 provenance), the public RuneLite client skill calculator enums, and local skilling-resource-details.json. Supplemental source descriptions are concise original summaries, with per-item Wiki article URLs retained in the data. No network requests or price lookups occur on hover. Source references: https://github.com/runelite/runelite/tree/runelite-parent-1.13.2/runelite-client/src/main/java/net/runelite/client/plugins/skillcalculator/skills and https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/ . Wiki Farming records were retrieved through the MediaWiki query API on 16 October 2026. Per-plant oldid references identify the exact stage-XP source revision. Supplemental source summaries avoid location and drop-rate estimates.
