# Production chains and shopping

The original direct-material allocation API remains available. `allocateChained` adds a producer-before-consumer order for explicitly selected recipes, using exact integer quantities and the observed base level for each skill. Inputs and intermediates are consumed once; deterministic products become forecast balances available to subsequent selected steps. Cycles and numeric overflow fail rather than silently producing invalid totals.

`ShoppingPlan` recursively expands a chosen final method through the player's selected, unlocked producer methods. It consumes existing stock before purchasing inputs, reconciles shared inputs into one shopping list, retains surplus products, and records XP per skill. `shoppingForTarget` chooses the smallest final action count whose same-skill XP, including upstream conversions, covers the remaining target. For example, five cut-and-bracelet conversions yield 1012.5 Crafting XP; the calculator does not request eleven bracelets while ignoring the cutting XP. Cross-skill producer XP remains separate.

The catalog contains 210 entries: 196 XP-bearing methods and 14 unfinished-potion preparation steps. 116 entries have explicit outputs. The native calculator's per-item units are retained for arrows/darts rather than interpreting its UI batch as one modelled item. The fourteen standard unfinished potions consume one clean herb plus one vial of water, produce one unfinished potion and award zero XP; their levels and preparation recipe are checked against the primary Wiki calculator module. No cooking success, chemistry proc, material saving, refund or other stochastic output is inferred. Moulds, tools, thread and access checks remain method/preparation requirements; the shopping list is a material list, not a claim that every activity unlock has been completed.

The bank UI enables chains by default, exposes the actual ordered conversions, retains direct mode, and shows consolidated quantities and item-source links. A player's real bank snapshot is never mutated by a forecast. Zero-XP preparation is excluded from final XP-method selectors. Unchecking a producer makes the shopping list buy its intermediate instead.

Validation covers reversed producer/consumer selection, gem-to-jewellery flows, herb cleaning and unfinished potions, competing feather uses, shared inputs, locked producers and final methods, per-skill XP, upstream-XP target minimization, absence of invented cooking/Prayer output, and checked overflow without mutation of the input bank. Existing direct-mode checks continue to pass.
